Cards

Flexible card components with media and content areas.

Card Title

This is a basic card with some text content. Cards are great for displaying information in a contained format.

Card with Image

Code Example

<!-- Basic Card -->
<div class="ax-card">
  <div class="ax-card__body">
    <h3 class="ax-card__title">Title</h3>
    <p class="ax-card__text">Content here</p>
    <button class="ax-btn ax-btn--primary">Action</button>
  </div>
</div>

<!-- Card with Media -->
<div class="ax-card">
  <div class="ax-card__media">
    <img src="image.jpg" alt="Description">
  </div>
  <div class="ax-card__body">
    <!-- content -->
  </div>
</div>

<!-- Card with Footer -->
<div class="ax-card">
  <div class="ax-card__body">
    <!-- content -->
  </div>
  <div class="ax-card__footer">
    <button>Action</button>
  </div>
</div>
